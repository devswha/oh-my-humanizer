# Pattern 9 (en): Negative Parallelisms — Success Case

## Input Text

> This initiative is not just a policy change but a fundamental reimagining of how cities approach affordable housing. It goes beyond mere infrastructure investment to become a statement about equity and belonging. The plan is not merely an update to existing zoning codes but also a bold declaration that every resident deserves access to green space. In short, the project transcends budgetary considerations to become a vision for a more humane urban future.

## Expected Output

> This initiative changes policy and fundamentally rethinks how cities approach affordable housing. Its infrastructure investment expresses a commitment to equity and belonging. The plan updates existing zoning codes and declares that every resident deserves access to green space. The project looks beyond budgets toward a more humane urban future.

## Applied Pattern

- Pattern 9 (Negative Parallelisms): Four negative-then-positive constructions in one paragraph — "not just...but a fundamental reimagining," "goes beyond...to become," "not merely...but also a bold declaration," and "transcends...to become." Each states what the initiative is not before stating what it is, adding rhetorical weight without adding information.

## Judgment

**Success** — Four negative-then-positive frames repeat in one paragraph. The rewrite keeps the policy change and investment as well as the larger claims about housing, equity, green space and the city's future. It does not turn those claims into invented project specifications.
