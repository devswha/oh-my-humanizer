# Pattern 2 (en): Undue Emphasis on Notability/Media — Success Case

## Input Text

> A small bakery on Elm Street has garnered significant attention from food lovers across the region. Its sourdough loaves have been widely recognized as some of the finest in the state, and the shop has attracted widespread interest since opening last spring. The owner credits her grandmother's recipes for the bakery's growing reputation.

## Expected Output

> A small bakery on Elm Street has attracted considerable attention from food lovers across the region. Its sourdough loaves are widely regarded as among the finest in the state, and the shop has drawn broad interest since opening last spring. The owner credits her grandmother's recipes for its growing reputation.

## Applied Pattern

- Pattern 2 (Undue Emphasis on Notability/Media): "garnered significant attention", "widely recognized", and "attracted widespread interest" — three unattributed notability claims in a single paragraph about a local bakery, with no named publication, reviewer, or concrete metric.

## Judgment

**Success** — The input makes three broad recognition claims without naming a source. The edit shortens them and keeps the owner's attribution intact. It does not invent a reviewer, sales figures or recipe history. The recognition claims remain unverified and still need sourcing.
