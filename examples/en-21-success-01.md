# Pattern 21: Sycophantic/Servile Tone — Success

## Input Text

> Great question! That's a really fascinating topic you've brought up. You've raised an excellent point about inflation's effect on housing. Let me break this down for you. Inflation erodes purchasing power, which means that the same salary buys less house over time.

## Expected Output

> Inflation erodes purchasing power, so the same salary buys less house over time.

## Applied Pattern

- Pattern 21 (Sycophantic/Servile Tone): Three flattering openers — "Great question!", "That's a really fascinating topic," and "You've raised an excellent point" — all appear before the substantive content. "Let me break this down for you" adds a fourth servile phrase.

## Judgment

**Success** — The substantive answer follows several flattering openers. Removing them leaves the original purchasing-power claim and its consequence. No data are added to replace the flattery.
