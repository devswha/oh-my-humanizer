# Pattern 33 (en): Definitional-Metaphor Equation — Success Case

## Input Text

> Symmetry is the architecture of trust. Consistency is the currency of every relationship that lasts. Teams move faster when they stop second-guessing each handoff.

## Expected Output

> Symmetry underpins trust. Every lasting relationship depends on consistency. Teams move faster when they stop second-guessing each handoff.

## Applied Pattern

- Pattern 33 (Definitional-Metaphor Equation): two abstract copula equations (`architecture of trust`, `currency of every relationship`) recur in the same passage.

## Judgment

**Success** — Two abstract metaphor equations recur. The rewrite simplifies them while keeping symmetry, consistency in every lasting relationship, and the separate handoff claim. It does not invent a causal link between those claims.
