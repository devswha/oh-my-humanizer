# Pattern 30: Rhetorical Question Openers — Success

## Input Text

> So does remote work really boost productivity? Studies suggest a 13% average productivity gain for remote workers. The main drivers are time saved on commuting and improved focus.
>
> But there are concerns too. Why are some companies pushing employees back to the office? The answer comes down to collaboration and culture. Without face-to-face interaction, casual conversation and serendipitous idea exchange become harder.
>
> So what does the future of work look like? Most experts now agree on a hybrid model.

## Expected Output

> Studies suggest a 13% average productivity gain for remote workers. The main drivers are time saved on commuting and improved focus.
>
> Some companies are pushing employees back to the office over concerns about collaboration and culture. Without face-to-face interaction, casual conversation and serendipitous idea exchange become harder.
>
> Most experts now agree on a hybrid model.

## Applied Pattern

- Pattern 30 (Rhetorical Question Openers): Two of three paragraphs open with "So..." rhetorical questions, and the middle paragraph adds another with "Why...?". Each question is answered within the same paragraph.

## Judgment

**Success** — The rewrite removes questions whose answers follow in the same paragraph. It retains the studies' tentative 13% claim, its stated drivers, the collaboration concern and the majority expert view. No uncertain result becomes a proven outcome.
