# Pattern 25: Metronomic Paragraph Structure — Success

## Input Text

> Remote work has grown rapidly since 2020. A Gallup survey found that 56% of U.S. workers now have jobs that can be done remotely. This shift represents a fundamental change in workplace culture.
>
> Coworking spaces have emerged as a popular alternative. WeWork reported a 30% increase in memberships last year. This trend signals a new model for how people balance flexibility and social connection.
>
> Video conferencing technology has improved significantly. Zoom's daily meeting participants grew from 10 million in 2019 to over 300 million by 2023. This development underscores the central role of technology in enabling distributed teams.

## Expected Output

> A Gallup survey found that 56% of U.S. workers now have jobs that can be done remotely. Remote work has grown rapidly since 2020, marking a fundamental change in workplace culture.
>
> Coworking spaces offer a popular alternative and a new way to balance flexibility with social connection. WeWork reported a 30% increase in memberships last year.
>
> Video conferencing technology has improved significantly and plays a central role in enabling distributed teams: Zoom's daily meeting participants grew from 10 million in 2019 to over 300 million by 2023.

## Applied Pattern

- Pattern 25 (Metronomic Paragraph Structure): All three paragraphs follow the identical template: general claim, then statistic as evidence, then interpretive "significance" sentence. The structure is claim-evidence-significance repeated three times without variation.

## Judgment

**Success** — The three input paragraphs repeat claim, statistic and significance. The rewrite leads with data, then varies where evidence appears. Gallup and WeWork remain the sources of their figures; the Zoom comparison and all three interpretations stay intact.
