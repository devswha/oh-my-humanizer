# Pattern 10 (en): Rule of Three Overuse — Success Case

## Input Text

> The program fosters creativity, innovation, and collaboration among early-career professionals. Over the course of eight weeks, participants gain inspiration, practical skills, and lasting connections that extend well beyond the cohort. The result is a more dynamic, inclusive, and forward-thinking community of practice that continues to grow each year.

## Expected Output

> The program encourages creativity and innovation among early-career professionals. It also encourages collaboration. During eight weeks, participants gain inspiration and practical skills, along with lasting connections beyond the cohort. The resulting community of practice is more dynamic and inclusive, looks ahead, and keeps growing each year.

## Applied Pattern

- Pattern 10 (Rule of Three Overuse): Three triple-item lists in three consecutive sentences — "creativity, innovation, and collaboration," "inspiration, practical skills, and lasting connections," and "dynamic, inclusive, and forward-thinking." Every sentence lands on exactly three items, creating a metronomic rhythm.

## Judgment

**Success** — Three successive triads give the passage a repeated rhythm. The rewrite redistributes their content across sentences. It retains all benefits, the eight-week duration and the community's yearly growth without inventing program outcomes.
