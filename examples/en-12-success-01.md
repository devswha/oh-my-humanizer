# Pattern 12 (en): False Ranges — Success Case

## Input Text

> The platform serves everyone from busy executives to stay-at-home parents, spanning everything from project management to personal wellness. Whether you are a seasoned entrepreneur or a first-time freelancer, the tools adapt to your workflow. The features range from simple task tracking to advanced AI-driven analytics, making it suitable for individuals and teams alike.

## Expected Output

> The platform serves everyone, including busy executives and stay-at-home parents. It covers project management and personal wellness, and its tools adapt to the workflows of seasoned entrepreneurs and first-time freelancers alike. Features include simple task tracking and advanced AI-driven analytics, making it suitable for individuals and teams.

## Applied Pattern

- Pattern 12 (False Ranges): Three decorative ranges in one paragraph — "from busy executives to stay-at-home parents," "from project management to personal wellness," and "from simple task tracking to advanced AI-driven analytics." None of these pairs define a meaningful spectrum; the endpoints are chosen for rhetorical breadth, not informational precision.

## Judgment

**Success** — The passage uses several ranges to describe audiences and features. The rewrite names those groups and features directly while preserving the claim of broad coverage. Product tiers, prices and customer statistics cannot be inferred from this input.
