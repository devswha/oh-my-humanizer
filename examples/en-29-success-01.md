# Pattern 29: False Nuance (Retroactive Reframing) — Success

## Input Text

> A four-day workweek raises employee satisfaction. Actually, it's more nuanced than that — a four-day week can improve satisfaction in certain contexts while presenting challenges in others, and the net effect depends on organizational culture and individual work styles.

## Expected Output

> A four-day workweek can improve satisfaction in some contexts and present challenges in others. Its net effect depends on organizational culture and individual work styles.

## Applied Pattern

- Pattern 29 (False Nuance): "Actually, it's more nuanced than that" introduces conditions that qualify the opening claim. The conditions must survive even if the transition is shortened.

## Judgment

**Success (limited wording repair)** — The edit removes the staged self-correction and retains its substantive qualification. Culture, work styles and contextual limits change the opening generalization, so they cannot be deleted as false nuance. This case illustrates a preservation boundary, not a claim that those caveats lack content.
